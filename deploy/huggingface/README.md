# Quantix — AI-powered science & maths lab

Free tools for students, in English and हिन्दी:

* **Measure** real objects from a photo (coin / card scale) with their mensuration formulas
* **Formula Library** — 1,022 formulas, PDFs and a step-by-step calculator
* **Periodic Table**, **Physics Lab** simulations, **Equation Solver** (photo of handwriting → solved / plotted),
  **Unit Converter**, **Graph Plotter**, and shape recognition with a rotatable 3D model
